Experimenting with starting the week with weeknotes, perhaps as a momentum bump into the coming week. I say experimenting, it's just this one. Easing back in by talking about stuff I've consumed.

I finished a really interesting book this week - '[Every Living Thing][book]' by Jason Roberts. A biography of [Linneaus][linneaus] and [De Buffon][buffon], that ranged much wider than just biographical details, though it was great on the widely different outlooks and characters. I found myself relating so much back to my enjoyment of classifying and categorizing. Would recommend.
